Graduation & diplomas — Graduation Roadmap

Claim on the hubStatusWhat was found · sourceInitials
Traditional diploma = 24 Carnegie units (9th-grade cohorts 2018 and after).VerifiedSREB Mississippi readiness summary and the Natchez-Adams district diploma page both list 24. Note: the Cornell/LII copy of Accreditation Appendix 8 shows 26 with 7.5 electives — an older version; MDE's current table is 24.
Subject breakdown: English 4, Math 4, Science 3, Social Studies 3.5, Health ½, PE ½, Technology/CS 1, Arts 1, College & Career Readiness 1, Electives 5.5.CorrectedThe roadmap had omitted the College & Career Readiness credit and showed 6.5 electives. Now matches SREB's breakdown.
Career & Technical endorsement: 26 units, GPA 2.5, 4 units in one CTE program, ACT WorkKeys Silver, plus dual credit / work-based learning / national credential.CorrectedGPA, WorkKeys and the credential alternatives were added (Natchez-Adams diploma options page, mirroring MDE).
Academic endorsement: 26 units, GPA 2.5, ACT 17 English / 19 Math, one AP/IB/dual-credit course (C or better).VerifiedNatchez-Adams diploma page; LII Appendix 8 (same ACT subscores and advanced-credential rule).
Distinguished Academic endorsement: 28 units, GPA 3.0, ACT 18 English / 22 Math, AP/IB/dual credit with a B or better.VerifiedNatchez-Adams diploma page.
End-of-course assessments required in Algebra I, Biology I, English II, U.S. History (with approved alternatives).VerifiedLII Appendix 8 (Accreditation Policies). The specific alternate routes (ACT cut scores, course-grade combinations) are not listed on the hub — the counselor's current MDE guidance governs.
A student may attend public school until age 21, so a 5-year plan is possible.ConfirmMiss. Code §37-15-9 grants free attendance to residents under 21; local practice on enrolling 18–20-year-old newcomers varies. Confirm the district's position and write it into the roadmap note.
Seal of Biliteracy: English via ELPT 4 in all domains / English II score 4 / ACT English 18 / SAT 320 / AP English 3; other language via AP 3, AAPPL or STAMP Intermediate-Mid in all four skills, ASLPI 3, or a portfolio.VerifiedOxford School District's Seal of Biliteracy page (state criteria, adopted 2019). Confirm Laurel participates and which portfolio languages it accepts.

The ELPT — family guide, LSP builder, compliance calendar

Claim on the hubStatusWhat was found · sourceInitials
Exit = Level 4 or higher in each ELPT domain on the same administration.VerifiedState Board of Education decision, June 19, 2025 (reported by Citizen Portal); also the Seal of Biliteracy English criterion. An alternate assessment option exists for students with disabilities.
ELPT summative window is March 1 – April 16, 2027; screener available July 20, 2026 – May 27, 2027.CorrectedThe hub said "February–March." Changed on the family guide, the compliance calendar and the LSP notes to match the 2026–27 Statewide Testing Calendar.
Overall labels Emerging / Progressing / Proficient.VerifiedLaurel School District EL Plan SY25–26 uses the three ELPA21 proficiency-determination labels.
Monitoring after exit lasts four years, with grades and assessments reviewed every nine weeks.VerifiedLaurel School District EL Plan SY25–26 ("monitor their academic progress for 4 years"; review "every 9 weeks").
Long-term EL = 6+ years receiving services.VerifiedLaurel School District EL Plan SY25–26.
Screening within 30 calendar days at the start of the year; 10 school days for later enrollees; parent notification on the same clock.CorrectedThe hub said "2 weeks" (the ESSA ceiling). Laurel's plan uses 10 school days; the calendar and family guide now say so.
Title III immigrant student = age 3–21, not born in a state, fewer than 3 full academic years in U.S. schools.VerifiedLaurel School District EL Plan SY25–26 (ESSA definition).
LSP contents: demographics, first U.S. enrollment date, yearly ELPT scores, classroom accommodations/modifications, state-testing accommodations, signatures of the Student Evaluation Team.VerifiedLaurel School District EL Plan SY25–26. The LSP Builder includes all six plus goals; the district form, if any, governs the layout.
First-year ELs and the ELA state test (exemption or participation rule).ConfirmNeither the MTAM nor the district plan addresses it in the sections checked. The compliance calendar says "check the current MDE rule" — have the DTC state it.
ELPT participation: 95% is an accountability requirement.ConfirmTrue for federal accountability participation generally; confirm how MDE's model applies it to ELPT specifically.
§15 EL Progress indicator in the state accountability model.ConfirmReferenced on the calendar by name; the current business rules (growth-to-target by years in program) are MDE's and change — attach the current year's rule.

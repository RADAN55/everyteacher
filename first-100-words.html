For teachers

These 100 words were chosen from what newcomers actually need to get through a school day — people, places, the schedule, instructions, lunch and the words on a report card — not from a frequency list. Ten a day for two weeks, with the Quiz mode as the exit ticket, works well in the Newcomer lane. Print with the browser's print button for a 4-per-row flashcard sheet. Pronunciation uses the device's built-in voice (works on Chromebooks and phones; needs sound on).

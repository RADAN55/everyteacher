In December a classroom of English learners holds more geography than the textbook. This special edition treats what students already know as curriculum. Every page is about customs: the lantern, the food, the skates, the songs. Nobody is asked what a family believes.

Who it is for

Every teacher in the building.

EL teachers

The tour, the 90-minute lesson in four lanes, the countdown calendar and the ELPA21 practice insert with listening scripts and scoring guides.

Elementary teachers

Four coloring pages with a sentence to trace, listen-and-circle practice for K–2, and a reading page for grades 3–5.

ELA teachers

Every activity matched to the Mississippi ELA standards, and eight whole-class activities built on the edition's texts.

Every other subject

One activity each for math, science, social studies, art, music, PE, career and technical, technology, the library and homeroom, plus state test practice for math, science and history.

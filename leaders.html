The two-week path

For the federal programs director who has a monitoring visit, a board meeting, or a new superintendent on the calendar.

Days 1–2 · Know where you standRun the self-audit with your EL teacher and one principal. Print the action plan. Assign owners that afternoon.
Days 3–5 · Close the red itemsLetters in the home language, the monitored-student roster, LSP accommodations in two columns. Every template is on the hub.
Days 6–9 · Get the money rightCheck the Title III split. Move any required service off the grant in the next budget amendment.
Days 10–14 · Tell the storyLoad the dashboard, fill the board brief, present the deck. Ask for the half-time coordinator.

Why this exists

Most Mississippi districts have one EL teacher and no coordinator

The compliance work belongs to nobody, the data never reaches the board, and the first time anyone looks closely is when the monitor or the complaint arrives. These tools give the federal programs director, the principal, and the superintendent the same checklist the EL specialist uses, in the language each of them reads.

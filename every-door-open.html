Introduction

How to Use This Book

Multilingual learners spend most of their day in English, math, science and social studies classes. This guide gathers what the research says works in each of those rooms and turns it into steps a teacher can use on Monday.

Each chapter has two parts. The research names five moves and the studies behind them. Teacher steps breaks each move into four actions, with supports for each language level.

Students don't need to finish learning English first.

The National Academies found that language and content grow together. Holding back grade-level content until English is "ready" slows both down.

    A two-week plan and every source in this book

    1. 1Choose one subject chapter and one move.
    2. 2Plan the four steps into your next lesson.
    3. 3Add the support for each language level in your room.
    4. 4Use the same move every day for two weeks.
    5. 5Check exit tickets, then add a second move.

    Ask your EL specialist to co-plan a unit, model a move in your room, or help set up leveled frames for your students.

Facilitator guide

Who this is for

A department, a grade-level team, or a whole faculty of content teachers who have English learners on their rosters. It works best with eight to twelve people, a copy of the book each, and a fixed slot: the first PLC meeting of every other week for a semester, or every week for two months.

The shape of every session

0–5Opener. One question, everybody answers in a sentence. No reading required to answer it.5–10Bring back. Last week's try-it: two people describe what happened, in two minutes each. Evidence beats opinion: a student sentence, a photo of the board, an exit ticket.10–30Discuss. Four questions on the six strategies read. The facilitator's job is to keep the talk on what we would do in our rooms, not on whether the book is right.30–40Choose. Each teacher names the one strategy they will try before the next session, the class, and the day. Say it out loud; write it in the log.40–45Exit. One sentence each: the thing I am taking into my room.

Norms that make it work

  • Read before you come; the discussion assumes the six strategies. Fifteen to twenty pages a session.
  • One try-it per teacher per session, in a class that has at least one English learner. Small is fine; done is the point.
  • Bring evidence, not a verdict. "Here is what the two newcomers wrote" is worth more than "it went well."
  • The EL specialist attends as a colleague, not the expert. The questions are written so that the content teacher's knowledge of the content is what matters.

Credit and evidence

Eight sessions at 45 minutes is six contact hours; the try-it log and the certificate document the application. For Mississippi Title III professional-development reporting, keep the sign-in sheets, the session dates and a copy of each teacher's log. The EL Program Compliance Calendar has the reporting dates.

Pairing with the hub

Each session names one EL Publishing resource that puts the strategy in a content teacher's hands the same day: the Sentence-Frame Bank, Cognate Hunt, Accommodations Quick Cards, Co-Teaching Lesson Plan, EL Assistants, and Sheltered, the twelve-minute simulation that makes a good session zero.

Sentence-frame bank

150 frames by language function — narrate, inform, explain, argue, and classroom talk — and by level 1–5, each with a Spanish gloss so students know what they are saying. Filter to today's function and your students' levels, tick the ones you want, copy them into your slide.

How to use frames without getting stuck on them

Post two or three, not twelve. Say the frame aloud and have the class echo it before the first student uses it. Pair a Level 1–2 frame with a Level 3–4 frame on the same slide so students pick their own step up. Take frames down after three weeks of success in that function — the goal is the language, not the scaffold. The underlined words are the slots students fill; the Spanish gloss tells a newcomer what the English frame means, so they can use it on day one.

Functions follow the four WIDA Key Language Uses; levels follow ELPA21 proficiency levels (1 beginning – 5 advanced).

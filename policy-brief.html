What this is

One page a month

Who it's for

Federal programs directors, EL coordinators, principals, and the superintendent who has to answer a board question in two sentences.

How it's made

Watched weekly, written monthly

A standing weekly scan of MDE, ALSDE, USED, OCR, and Congress feeds the issue. Every claim is checked against the primary document before it's posted; corrections are logged on the Fact-Check page.

How the hub is built and kept accurate

Method

Single-file pages, no tracking

Every resource is a self-contained web page that prints cleanly, works on a Chromebook, and keeps your data in your browser. The only analytics is a privacy-respecting page counter with no cookies. Nothing you type in a tool is sent anywhere.

Accuracy

Primary sources, logged corrections

State-specific claims are checked against the current MDE documents: the Testing Accommodations Manual (Feb. 19, 2026), the 2026–27 testing calendar, the June 2025 exit standard, and district EL plans. Corrections are recorded publicly on the Fact-Check page with the source and the date. If you find an error, email it; it will be fixed and logged.

Review dates

Dated, and re-dated

Compliance pages carry a "last reviewed" date in their footer or header. The Policy Brief is reviewed monthly; the Compliance Calendar and accommodations cards are reviewed when MDE publishes a new manual or calendar, and at least each August.

Accessibility

The hub aims to meet WCAG 2.1 AA. Pages use semantic headings and landmarks, visible keyboard focus, text contrast of at least 4.5:1 on body text, labels on every form control, and no content that depends on color alone (lane colors are always paired with a lane name). Tools work without a mouse, and the interactive pages work at 200% zoom and at phone width. Several pages include text-to-speech. Translations are machine-assisted and reviewed for school vocabulary; native-speaker corrections are welcome. If something is hard to use with your assistive technology, email richard.spanishteacher@gmail.com with the page and the barrier, and it will be fixed.

Privacy and student data

No page on this hub collects, stores, or transmits student information. Tools that generate documents (LSP Builder, Student Profile, Newcomer Intake, Outcomes Dashboard, Self-Audit) run entirely in your browser; what you type stays on your device unless you print, copy, or export it yourself. The AI-prompt tools (EL Assistants, EL Bridge) build prompts you run in your own AI account, and every prompt tells you not to paste student names, IDs, grades, or plan content. Showcase submissions are reviewed by hand before posting, and anything identifying a student is removed.

For the teacher: how to use the map, and what each station covers

Fourteen stations in the order a beginner's grammar course usually runs: the capital letter, the eight parts of speech with determiners and tenses, agreement, the sentence, and punctuation. Each station is Know it (a short lesson in plain English with the Spanish term), See it (examples with the target underlined), and Try it (eight self-checking items: tap the word, choose, or type). Seven of eight earns the stamp. Progress stays on the device.

In class

Project the X-ray for a two-minute opener: read the sentence, turn on the X-ray, ask "what is quickly doing?" The map is a natural intervention menu: a student whose writing drops the -s goes to station 7; a student writing i goes to station 1. Newcomers travel the Words line first (1–5) with Español on; Emerging students add the Verbs line; Progressing students should clear the whole map and then move to the Writing Studio.

Alignment

MS ELP Standard 10 (accuracy with standard English), with ELP 9 (coherent writing) at stations 12–13. ELPA21 writing items for editing words and sentences draw on exactly these features: capitals, plurals, agreement, tense, articles, and end punctuation.

Is this plan compliant?

—

    Checks run on what is typed above and nothing leaves your browser. A green line means the plan documents it; it does not mean the district did it — the dates are yours to stand behind.

    Components follow Mississippi district EL plans (demographics, first U.S. enrollment date, yearly ELPT scores, classroom accommodations, state-testing accommodations, Student Evaluation Team signatures) plus goals, nine-weeks review and the identification trail that an OCR or MDE review asks for first. Exit: Level 4 or higher in each ELPT domain on the same administration (State Board, June 2025). Monitoring: four years, reviewed every nine weeks. Accommodation allowability on MAAP and ELPT follows the current Mississippi Testing Accommodations Manual; no primary-language support or bilingual dictionary on the ELPT. Your district's form, if it has one, governs the layout; this builder produces the content.

What this is

EL Publishing is where I put the materials I build for my own classroom. Everything is designed around one idea: English learners do not need easier content, they need the language that carries it.

The resources assume a real high school — mixed proficiency levels in one room, content teachers with thirty students and one planning period, and a compliance file that has to hold up. Every packet includes a differentiated version for newcomers through challenge level, because that is what a single class period actually requires.

Using these in your building

  • Print, copy and distribute anything here for your students and your faculty.
  • Adapt the wording for your own school, state or grade band.
  • Share the link with a colleague, a department or a district PD session.

Two cautions

  • Compliance content is written to Mississippi requirements — MTAM, LSP, ELPA21. Check it against your own state's before you republish it.
  • Illustrations in the Hispanic Heritage gallery are AI-generated interpretations, not photographs. Say so when you display them, and send students to the linked source for verified facts.

Teacher Guide

Storm Lens turns the real classroom into the vocabulary list. Nothing leaves the device — no account, no server, no photos uploaded anywhere.

What it does

  • Lens: the Chromebook/phone camera recognizes everyday objects (80 object classes run entirely in the browser). Tap a word → hear it, see syllables, Spanish, cognate flag, and four-lane sentence frames.
  • Say it back: the student speaks; the browser's speech recognition checks the word and each frame. Correct attempts earn stars.
  • Word Wall: saved words keep the student's own photo. Export CSV for Ellevation/portfolio evidence or print flashcards.
  • Practice: photo flashcards from the student's own wall — production before recognition.
  • Missions: scavenger hunts by category (things you can eat, things with a screen…).

Lanes

N · NewcomerE · EmergingP · ProgressingC · Challenge

Set the lane in the top bar. Every word card shows all four frames, but the selected lane is marked for the student. Newcomer frames are label-level ("This is a chair."), Emerging adds location and description, Progressing asks for purpose with because, Challenge asks for comparison and a complete two-sentence description.

A 15-minute routine

  1. Hunt (5 min): pairs walk the room and save 5 new words. One holds the device, one says the word first.
  2. Say (5 min): each partner earns 3 stars on their lane frame for every word.
  3. Share (5 min): Practice round on the projector — class says the word before the photo reveals it.

Tips & troubleshooting

  • Works best in Chrome/Edge on Chromebooks, Android, and desktop. Speech recognition needs a Chromium browser and a path to Google's speech service; if the district filter blocks it, Storm Lens switches to Record & compare automatically (the student records, hears the model and themselves, and self-checks). On iPhone Safari, Record & compare is the normal mode.
  • Mic not working? Run the Device check below: it asks for the microphone, shows a live level bar, and names the exact problem (blocked by the lock icon, in use by another tab, no mic, or speech service blocked).
  • The first load downloads the recognition model (a few MB). After that it is cached.
  • If the camera is blocked, the student can use Upload photo or Type it — the whole word card still works.
  • Not every object is recognized (the model knows 80 common things). "Type it" covers 150+ more classroom words, and any typed word still gets audio and frames.
  • Word Wall is saved in this browser only (localStorage). Export CSV before clearing a shared device.

Use / whyfor, to, because, so3 · 7

3 of 4 = on track for Progressing; 4 of 4 with because = Challenge-lane evidence. The app auto-scores the four points from the transcript and awards 2 stars at 3+.

What "depth" looks like on one word

  • Word in action — a verb collocation (raise your hand, zip up your coat) and an example sentence, both speakable and speech-checked.
  • Say it right — rule-based pronunciation coaching keyed to Spanish-to-English transfer (initial S+consonant, V/B, TH, SH/CH, H, short I, final consonants and -S, English R).
  • One or many — regular and irregular plurals; non-count nouns flagged.
  • Word family — compounds and related forms, cognate flag.
  • Quick check — a meaning question with same-category distractors.
  • Write it — sentence check: uses the word, capital, end mark, 5+ words, and because/so/but in Progressing/Challenge lanes.

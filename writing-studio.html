The Writing Studio: write for a 4 or a 5.

Eight lessons at the chalkboard with Mr. Castillo. He writes, he explains, he asks you for one line and checks it, and at the end he reads you a model answer and tells you why it scores. Use it alone on a phone, or project it and teach from it.

Mr. Castillo is an animated teacher with a synthesized voice; the lessons were written and edited by Richard A. Daniel, M.Ed. What you write stays on your device.

For the teacher: how to use it, the eight lessons, and the full script

Each lesson is an I-do / we-do / you-do at the board. Mr. Castillo models the move on the chalkboard while thinking aloud, asks the class to choose (the pick items), asks each student for one line in the notebook and checks it against a short list of features (the ask items), and in lesson 8 runs a full timed response with a plan box, a clock, a self-score and a level-5 model with the reasons it scores. Progress and the student's writing stay in the browser; a Copy my writing button lets students paste a response into a document for you.

Two ways to run it

Projector, you teach. Press Projector for larger chalk, and Mute (I'll teach) if you want to be the voice: the board still writes each line when you press Next, the caption shows you his words, and the script below is yours to read or ignore. Students write their lines on paper; press Show a model when you want his line on the board. Alone, on a phone. He speaks, writes and checks; a student can replay any line and slow him down.

What the lessons teach, in order

Alignment

TargetThe features a top-level ELPA21 writing response shows: a claim that answers the prompt in the prompt's words; two reasons, each with an example or a result; organizing transitions with correct punctuation; the other side acknowledged; a conclusion; a formal register when the task is an email; controlled sentences and clean mechanics. The lessons teach those features; they do not reproduce any scoring document.
MS ELP StandardsELP 3 (speak and write about topics), ELP 4 (construct claims and support them with reasoning and evidence), ELP 7 (adapt language choices to purpose and audience), ELP 9 (clear and coherent writing), ELP 10 (accuracy with standard English)
LanesWritten for Progressing students (ELPT 3–4) reaching for 4–5. Emerging students can follow with the captions on and the Slower setting; Challenge students should write the lesson-8 response to a second prompt and edit a partner's.

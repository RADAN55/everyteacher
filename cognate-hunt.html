    What this is, and what it is not

    1. Solid underline: a pair a bilingual teacher would vouch for on sight — from a list of 373 school words, science and history heaviest.
    2. Dotted underline: an ending pattern (-tion → -ción, -ity → -idad) says it is probably a cognate. Usually right. Check it before it goes on the wall.
    3. Red: a false friend. Library is not librería. Exit is not éxito.

    Nothing leaves your browser — the text is never sent anywhere. The lists are small and honest rather than large and guessy; if a word your students use is missing, send it and it goes in. This is the tool for Monday Move 10, Go on a Cognate Hunt.

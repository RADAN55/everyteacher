The lesson, in 20 minutes

  1. Teach the graph (3 min). Point to the solid part (first language, learned at home), the striped part (second language, learned later) and the number at the end (the total).
  2. Words to know (2 min). First language, second language, million, billion, total, with the Spanish for each. Watch “billion”: in Spanish, billón means a million million; an English billion is mil millones.
  3. Answer the questions (10 min). Five questions, from finding one number to explaining a reason, each with a sentence starter and room to write.
  4. Talk (5 min). “My home language is ___. It is / is not on the graph.” How many languages do you speak?
The big idea: about 3 of every 4 English speakers in the world learned English as a second language. Your students are joining the largest group of English speakers there is.

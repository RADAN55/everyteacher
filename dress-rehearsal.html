Dress Rehearsal: the device check.

Five short stations. Practice the headset, the microphone, typing, scrolling and checking your answers, so nothing on test day is new. About 20 minutes. Nothing you say or type leaves this device.

Directions:

Station 1 · Listening

Check your headset

On the test, every listening item plays through your headset. Make sure you can hear both sides and set the volume now, not on test day.

1. Which ear?

Put your headset on. Tap Play. A tone plays in one ear. Tap the ear you heard it in.

2. Listen for the number

A short announcement plays. You can play it two times, like on the test. Tap the room number you hear.

The voice is your device's built-in synthesized voice. If you hear nothing, turn up the volume, then tap Test audio.

Station 2 · Speaking

Talk into the microphone

On the test, nobody is in front of you. You talk to a microphone, and silence scores nothing. Practice talking the whole time.

1. Turn on the microphone

Your browser will ask. Choose Allow. Then say "testing, one, two, three" and watch the bar move.

2. Answer the question for 30 seconds

Start with: "I think… because… For example…"

0 of 30 seconds of talking

Your recording stays on this device. It is gone when you close the page.

Station 3 · Writing

Type without spell-check

The test has no spell-check and no red lines. A longer, organized answer scores higher than a short perfect one. Write more than you think.

Your school is choosing a new club. Which club should it be? Give two reasons.

0 words · goal: 50

    Station 4 · Reading

    Scroll, highlight, go back

    On the test the passage scrolls, and you can highlight. Read the question first, then go back to the text every time. The test is untimed.

    Tap a sentence to highlight it. Tap again to remove it.

    Station 5 · Every domain

    Flag it, then review it

    Not sure? Choose your best answer, flag it, and come back. Never leave a blank: a guess can score, a skip cannot.

    Use the numbers to move between questions. A gold dot means flagged.

    For teachers

    Run it in one class period

    Use it in the weeks before the window, on the same devices and headsets students will test with. Students work alone, at their own pace; the stations can go in any order. Nothing is sent anywhere, so there is no class report: students show you their pass card, or take a screenshot of it.

    StationWhat it rehearsesWatch for
    1 · HeadsetLeft and right channels, volume, a two-play limitHeadsets on backwards, volume at zero, students who replay without a purpose
    2 · MicrophoneAllowing the mic, talking for 30 seconds to no oneStudents who stop at 8 seconds; teach “Why? Like what?”
    3 · TypingNo spell-check, writing to length, capitals and end marksStudents who freeze on spelling; tell them spelling never stops the pen
    4 · ScrollScrolling a long passage, highlighting, going back for proofAnswers from memory; ask “Which sentence proves it?”
    5 · ReviewMoving between items, flagging, reviewing, no blanksStudents who submit with blanks; the page will not let them

    Chromebooks: if the microphone will not turn on, the device or district settings may block it for websites. Station 2 still works as a timed talk without the recording. Phones: tap Test audio once if the voice is silent. Pair it with Teaching the ELPT: the Workshop and the ELPT Practice Center.

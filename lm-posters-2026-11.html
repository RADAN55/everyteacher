1 · Listen Like a Partner

Discuss vs. collaborate, six listening moves with sentence frames, a listening target and “public voice.” For every classroom wall.

2 · Translate on Purpose

3 · You Belong Here

Becki Cohn-Vargas · “Fostering Authentic Belonging”

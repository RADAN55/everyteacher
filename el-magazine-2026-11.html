How teachers use it

Three ways in.

Teach the month

Pages 19–20 lay out a 90-minute lesson and a 15-lesson November sequence that strings the issue's readings, scripts and tasks into four weeks.

Pull one lab

Each lab stands alone: the Reading lab, Voice studio, Language workshop and Literary lens are two pages each, with Emerging and Progressing supports side by side.

Check the growth

Pages 21–24 hold an original check, a classroom rubric, answer keys, and guidance for feedback and reteaching.

Independent classroom edition

Original passages, scripts, and activities. Students answer in a notebook or on separate paper. Emerging and Progressing are planning labels, not fixed descriptions of ability. Not an official ELPA21 publication.

What it is

Resource-building, not consulting

EL Publishing builds the documents, tools, and training materials a district's EL program needs and hands them over, branded to the district, free to keep. It does not run your program, deliver your PD, or sign your compliance reports. Your people do that; they just stop starting from a blank page.

What it costs

Nothing, within limits

The hub is free. A 90-day engagement is offered at no charge to a small number of Mississippi districts a year, in exchange for feedback and permission to describe the work anonymously. Larger or longer engagements are discussed case by case.

Who it's for

Districts of 500 to 5,000

The ones with one EL teacher, no coordinator, a federal programs director wearing six hats, and a monitoring visit on the calendar. If that is you, this was built for you.

The 90-day engagement

Three months, three deliverables a month, one 45-minute call every two weeks. Everything below already exists on the hub in generic form; the engagement makes it yours.

What EL Publishing will not do
  • Hold or process student-level data. Templates are built on counts; your staff fill in names inside your systems.
  • Sign, certify, or represent your compliance status to MDE or anyone else.
  • Deliver PD for you. The module is built for your staff to deliver; a walk-through for the presenter is included.
  • Replace legal counsel. Everything cites the primary source; the interpretation is yours and your attorney's.

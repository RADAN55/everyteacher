Standing obligations — every day of the year

Enroll every child. No proof of immigration status, no Social Security number, no delay for missing records. Plyler v. Doe; MDE enrollment guidance.
Castañeda's three prongs. The EL program must be (1) based on sound educational theory, (2) implemented with adequate staff and resources, and (3) evaluated and modified when it is not working. Castañeda v. Pickard; EEOA §1703(f).
Meaningful access to the core curriculum. ELs are not segregated beyond what the program requires, and not excluded from honors, AP, CTE, athletics or clubs for language reasons. Title VI; Lau v. Nichols; 2015 USDOJ/OCR Dear Colleague Letter.
Language access for parents. Essential information — enrollment, discipline, special education, grades, meetings, the EL notices below — in a language parents understand, through competent interpreters and translators, free. Title VI; ESSA §1112(e)(4).
Qualified staff. EL instruction delivered by teachers trained for it; content teachers given the student's level and supports. Castañeda prong 2; MDE EL Guidelines.
Dual identification. An EL who may have a disability is evaluated in a timely way, in the right language; being an EL never delays a referral. IDEA; OCR guidance. ELs with IEPs still receive EL services.
Refusal is not removal. A parent who declines services still has a child who is an EL: annual ELPT, monitoring, accommodations and the district's duty to help remain. ESSA; OCR.
Supplement, not supplant. Title III dollars add to, never replace, the core EL program the district must fund itself. ESSA Title III §3115.
About this calendar. Deadlines come from ESSA §1112(e)(3) and §3113, Title VI of the Civil Rights Act, the EEOA, Lau, Castañeda, Plyler, the 2015 USDOJ/OCR Dear Colleague Letter, and the Mississippi Department of Education's EL Guidelines and accountability model, checked in October 2026 against the 2026–27 statewide testing calendar, the February 2026 Testing Accommodations Manual, the State Board's June 2025 exit standard and the Laurel School District EL Plan (SY25–26). MSIS data dates and Title III application dates are set each year by MDE — confirm them and write them in. This is a working checklist for coordinators, not legal advice; the district's Title III director and legal counsel are the authority.

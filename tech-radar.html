Six new tech tools for English learners.

Verified this month for grades 9–12, ELPA21 Levels 1–4. Every link below opens the real tool. Free, Chromebook-ready unless noted.

1

Gemini in Google Classroom · student study tools

Google · new Aug 2026 · free with every Workspace for Education edition · admin must enable for students

As of August 10, 2026 the Gemini tab in Classroom is open to students of all ages, not just 18+. A student hovers an assignment under "Due Soon," clicks "Learn with Gemini," and gets starter prompts built from that assignment's own text: make flashcards, quiz me, create a study guide, help me learn. With Gemini Notebook turned on, the same materials become a podcast-style audio overview or a visual diagram. It works in any Classroom-supported language where Gemini is available, so a Spanish-dominant student can ask for Spanish glosses. Your admin has to turn on Gemini in Classroom plus the Gemini app or Gemini Notebook for the student OU; nothing to install on the Chromebook.

Progressing lane · 90-minute block: post a 500-word leveled article as assignment material. Students (1) make ten flashcards with a Spanish gloss on each, (2) run "quiz me" and answer five questions in full sentences, (3) listen to the audio overview once with the text and once without, then (4) write a five-sentence summary in the assignment. Challenge lane adds "explain two things the audio left out."
Open Gemini in Classroom help & setup → Reading · Listening · Writing
2

Read Along in Google Classroom

Google · new June 2026 · free for every Workspace for Education school

Students read aloud to an AI "reading buddy" that gives real-time pronunciation help and asks comprehension questions, with Spanish home-language support on select titles. You see accuracy, fluency and comprehension per student. Needs a mic and a school Google account; admin may need to enable it on older domains.

Newcomer lane · 20-minute station: assign a Lexile-filtered text with Spanish support on. Student reads it aloud twice, then records a 30-second retell.
Open Read Along help & setup → Reading · Speaking · Listening
3

SpeakingMonkey

Independent · surfaced Sept 2026 · free · no account needed

Word-by-word pronunciation scoring against a native-speaker model, with A1–C2 sentence sets in 33 languages. Because there is no login it collects no student data, but it also has no formal student-privacy agreement — treat it as 13+ and teacher-supervised.

Emerging lane · 15-minute "sound of the week": students score five target sentences (/ʃ/ vs /tʃ/, final -ed), screenshot their best score into a Google Doc, then use the sentences in a partner dialogue.
Open SpeakingMonkey → Speaking · Listening
4

MinuteMic

Independent · Aug 2026 · free (Topic of the Day + two practice rounds daily)

Generates a topic, records 60 seconds of speech, and returns feedback on vocabulary, fluency, relevance and structure. Audio is discarded; only the transcript is analyzed. Optional sign-in; assume 13+.

Progressing → Challenge · ELPA21 speaking warm-up: one round cold, read the feedback, outline with a sentence frame, re-record. Challenge lane turns the transcript into a written paragraph.
Open MinuteMic → Speaking · Writing
5

Brisk Boost

Brisk Teaching · updated Sept 2026 · free teacher tier · COPPA-compliant

You build a Tutor, Writing Coach or Debate Partner activity once; students work through it in 50+ languages while you watch in real time, with safety flags and a pause button. The Aug 2026 update generates multi-level and multi-language versions of one resource in a single pass.

Emerging → Progressing: generate one text at Level 2 and Level 4 plus a Spanish version, then assign a Writing Coach that gives sentence-level feedback on a five-sentence response.
Open Brisk Boost → Reading · Writing
6

Google Translate · Practice mode

Google · April 2026 · free · phone app only (Android/iOS)

Practice mode now scores spoken pronunciation in English and Spanish with feedback like "some sounds were unclear." It is in the phone app, not the browser, so it belongs to homework or phone-permitted time.

Newcomer lane · survival language: a set of school phrases — office, cafeteria, bus — practiced on the phone at home, then performed live for a partner in class.
Read Google's announcement → Speaking · Listening

Watch list — close, but not this month

  • Microsoft Reading Coach — still free at coach.microsoft.com; the Dec 2025 LTI connects Canvas/Schoology, not Google Classroom, and the tool itself isn't new.
  • Gemini audio lessons in Google Classroom — teacher-side creation is still 18+ only, but the student side of Gemini in Classroom opened to all ages in August 2026 — see #1 above.
  • Learn Your Way (Google Labs) — grade-level text, slides and audio from a textbook; own-PDF upload still waitlisted.

Nothing Mississippi-specific surfaced this window. Dates for SpeakingMonkey and MinuteMic rest on Larry Ferlazzo's review posts; their own sites carry no changelog.

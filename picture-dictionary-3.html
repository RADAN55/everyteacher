    For the teacher: how to use the issue, and what is on each spread

    Issue 3 adds twelve spreads of twelve words and moves from places to ideas: the science lab, shapes and measurement, colors, the house and yard, daily-routine verbs, describing people, opposites in pairs, animals, safety and emergencies, the park, celebrations, and arts and music. Start with Issue 1 and Issue 2 if the student is brand new. Each word has the Spanish equivalent, a spoken pronunciation and a spoken example sentence. Tap a number on the picture or a word on the list and the two light up together.

    Four activities on every spread

    Listen and find plays a word; the student taps its number on the picture. Spell it plays a word; the student types it. Use it gives the example sentence with the word missing and three choices. Say it records the student saying the word and the sentence on the device's own microphone, plays it back next to the model, and never uploads anything. Eight of ten on Listen and find or Spell it stamps the spread.

    In class

    Project a spread for a two-minute warm-up: cover the list, tap numbers, class says the word. Print a spread (the print button hides everything but the two pages) for the wall or a newcomer's binder. Pairs: one student reads the Spanish, the partner says the English and taps it. The First 100 Words page covers survival words; this issue goes one room at a time.

    Alignment

    MS ELP Standards 1, 2 and 8 at Level 1–2: construct meaning from oral and visual input, participate in short exchanges, determine the meaning of words. ELPA21 Listen-and-Match items use exactly this skill: hear a word or sentence, pick the picture.

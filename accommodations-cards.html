Read before relying on the test column. "Allowed" reflects the author's understanding of the Mississippi Testing Accommodations Manual (MTAM) and the ELPA21/ELPT administration manual for 2026–27, as they apply to EL students without an IEP. Rules differ by test (MAAP ELA vs. math, ELPT by domain) and change yearly; the District Test Coordinator's current manual is the authority. Every state-test accommodation must appear in the student's LSP before the test window and be one the student uses routinely in class — an accommodation introduced on test day is both unhelpful and non-compliant. ELs with IEPs or 504 plans follow those plans in addition.

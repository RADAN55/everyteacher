Find your square. Start there.

Four subjects, four grade bands. Each square names what the language — not the content — is asking of your English learners at that point, two moves from the Monday Moves series, and the free resources that actually apply. Nothing here needs an account.

Sixteen squares. Narrow it to yours and the rest gets out of the way — or scroll the whole grid if you teach across bands. Every square ends with free resources from elsewhere — the Library of Congress, Stanford, PhET, WIDA — each one opened and checked before it was listed. Where one needs a free account, it says so.
